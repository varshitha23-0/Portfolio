Intelligent Foundations

As an undergraduate pursuing Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, I've become fascinated by how data, computation, and intelligent systems are transforming the way we solve problems. To me, data isn't just an input for machine learning—it is the foundation that enables intelligent systems to learn, adapt, and make informed decisions. This has sparked my interest in understanding not only how AI models work, but also the quality of the data they learn from, the assumptions behind them, and the impact they create.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of **AI, healthcare, and computational biotechnology**, where intelligent systems have the potential to accelerate discovery, improve decision-making, and contribute to solving real-world challenges. I enjoy exploring ideas across disciplines because some of the most meaningful innovations emerge when different fields come together.

Responsible & Sustainable Progress

While I'm excited about the rapid pace of technological advancement, I also believe every innovation carries responsibility. Throughout history, every era has been shaped by tools that expanded human potential. Today, AI is becoming one of those transformative tools. But as we build increasingly powerful systems, it's equally important to ask how we can make them more efficient, sustainable, and beneficial not only for people, but also for the environment.

Progress, to me, isn't simply about building bigger or faster systems; it's about creating smarter solutions that solve meaningful problems while respecting the world we live in. I strive to contribute to research that bridges AI with real-world applications, developing intelligent systems that are innovative, trustworthy, and designed with both humanity and sustainability in mind.

Primary Core Philosophy

At the end of the day, I don't just want to build intelligent systems—I want to understand the problems they're solving, why they work, how they can be improved, and how they can be developed responsibly. I believe the future of technology should be measured not only by what it can achieve, but also by how responsibly it achieves it, ensuring that innovation benefits both humanity and the planet we call home.
